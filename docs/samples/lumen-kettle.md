# Lumen K2 smart kettle — user guide

## Setup

Fill the kettle with at least 0.5 litres of water before the first use. Hold the power button for 5 seconds until the ring light blinks blue, then pair it in the Lumen app over Bluetooth.

## Temperature presets

The K2 has four presets: 70 °C for green tea, 80 °C for white tea, 90 °C for oolong and 100 °C for black tea and coffee. A custom preset can be set in the app in 1 °C steps between 40 °C and 100 °C.

## Keep warm

Keep-warm holds the chosen temperature for up to 2 hours, then the kettle switches off. It is enabled by a double press of the power button.

## Descaling

Descale every 4 weeks in hard-water areas. Mix one part citric acid with twenty parts water, boil it, leave it for 30 minutes, then rinse twice. The ring light turns orange when descaling is due.

## Warranty

The kettle has a 3-year warranty. The warranty does not cover damage from boiling it dry or from descaling with vinegar.
